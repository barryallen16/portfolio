tamil tokenizer

ai backend
problem
general subword tokenizers split tamil into excessive character fragments, ballooning sequence length and slowing model training compared to english.
tradeoff
writing a custom bpe engine in rust gained raw training speed, but meant building merge tracking and hugging face export tooling from scratch.
solution
1,135-token base vocabulary around tamil conjunct stems, aho-corasick parallel merges with rayon, and csr indexing. beats mbert compression by 36.9% with verified 100% decode fidelity.

triagemate

ai backend
problem

tradeoff

kaggle automation

backend ai
problem
long-running kaggle gpu experiments silently hit 12-hour session limits and drop unsaved checkpoints when sweeps stall.
tradeoff
streaming multi-gigabyte zip outputs straight to disk without memory buffers kept container footprints low, but required strict pre-flight disk space guards.
solution
fastapi control plane that shards sweeps across gpu/tpu kernels, sends telegram alerts at the 11-hour mark, and streams logs over websockets. evaluated speculative decoding on dual t4s (0.5567 draft acceptance).

fitcheck

frontend ai ui/ux
problem
multimodal outfit recommendation models are too large to run privately on mobile devices, while cloud services require uploading personal wardrobe photos.
tradeoff
distilling an 8b vision-language teacher into a 2b student saved 3.1x vram, but required 28k lora steps across 538k caption-qa pairs to preserve 92% scoring quality.
solution
offline-first react native android app with local sqlite, weather-aware outfit scoring via open-meteo, and on-device gguf inference. validated in a blind study and published in ieee tqcebt 2026.

sivabharani comments

ai backend
problem
locating specific community discussions across 1,400+ tamil tech videos meant manually scrubbing through hundreds of hours of raw audio.
tradeoff
relying on free-tier groq api instances kept compute free, but demanded custom worker scheduling to prevent 429 rate limit errors across 1.18m chunks.
solution
27-key worker pool with dynamic reset-header tracking and 65-second windowing. micro-chunk timestamp pinning indexes commenter names into meilisearch for sub-second jumping to exact video seconds.

txt

frontend backend ui/ux
problem
critical payments, gps coordinates, and images cannot be sent in rural or disaster zones when cellular data and wi-fi networks fail.
tradeoff
gsm-7 limits payloads to 153 characters per sms, requiring grayscale posterization down to 1.2 kb instead of transmitting full-color photos.
solution
offline android app that serializes photos, qr codes, and upi requests into chunked base36 packets with crc32 checksums. includes a native kotlin contentobserver module for sub-100ms sms arrival detection.
